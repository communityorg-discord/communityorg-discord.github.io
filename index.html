How we work

This site is ours. So is Community Organisation, so is USGRP, so are Spritely and the FSIT bots. The Fortnite Sprite Info & Trading server is not. Dion lives in England. Evan lives in Wales. The work is remote, on Discord, and on the systems we built together so a community can be run properly instead of from somebody’s memory.

We do not split “one of us owns it, the other helps.” Both of us founded Community Organisation. Both of us play in USGRP. Both of us built the bots. The only split is what each of us actually does with our hands.

Community Organisation

Community Organisation is what we started together. It is a Discord community with real structure behind it: departments, standards, staff, and tools we built in-house rather than a pile of public bots holding the place together. The idea is simple. A community with room for the next person, run like it matters.

That is the through-line of our past. We did not just sit in a server, and neither of us did the building alone. We built the organisation around it: how people join, how staff are recorded, how mail and sign-in work, how leadership is actually organised. Ownership sits with both of us. The day-to-day of the organisation is departments, not a group chat with extra steps.

The best-known community out of that is United States Government Roleplay (USGRP). It is a serious, story-driven US government roleplay Discord: departments, courts, law enforcement, an economy, and a chain of command people actually have to follow. We both play in it. Dion plays President. Evan plays White House Chief of Staff. The rest of the country is staffed by the community.

CO is being rebuilt: new look, same idea. The public site is communityorg.co.uk.

Fortnite Sprite Info & Trading

FSIT is a Discord for Fortnite sprites: collecting, indexing, trading, and a staff team that keeps it usable. It is not Community Organisation, and it is not USGRP. We did not make the server, and we do not own it. What is ours is the bots that run on it, and we built those together.

Spritely is the sprite bot: catalogue, who has what, the leaderboard, trading, and Sprite Operations requests. Utilities is the server bot: tickets, staff records, moderation, reports, and protection. Both are ours. Both are fan projects. They are not made by, endorsed by, or connected to Epic Games.

Spritely’s public page is sprites.codes.
