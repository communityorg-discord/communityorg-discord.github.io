Fortnite Sprite Info & Trading is a community Discord for Fortnite sprites — collecting, indexing, trading, and the staff team that keeps it usable.

Spritely is the sprite bot: catalogue, who has what, the leaderboard, trading, and Sprite Operations requests. Utilities is the server bot: tickets, staff records, moderation, reports, and the protection in the background.

Both are fan projects. They are not made by, endorsed by, or connected to Epic Games.

Spritely’s public page is sprites.codes.
