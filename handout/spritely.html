Indexing

Type it, pick it,
it is on your card.

01The sprite list is the catalogue — an unknown name is refused, never created. A collection where a typo can mint a sprite is a collection nobody can compare.
02The form list only offers the forms that sprite actually has, in the game's own words — Cheatmaster, Loot Hacker.
03The reply is only visible to them and says the running total: mastered, indexed, and the 61 they can get.
04And it checks the name again when it runs, not only in the pop-up list — so a form that does not exist is refused, and the reply says which forms do.

Somebody banned from indexing is told the reason on the spot, not left with a bot that looks broken.

The indexing ticket

Paste the table.
It reads it.

A member opens a sprite-index ticket and pastes the published table. Spritely reads every answered ticket within the minute — a member who has just pasted is sitting there looking at the channel.

→It names every line it could not read, not the first one — one round trip instead of three.
→On a phone the header wraps onto two lines — the bot copes: sixteen rows outvote one line.
→Other people's formats read too: shortcodes like :crown:, box-drawing bars, a table with its own key at the top, two tables side by side, Base / Gold / Cheat / Hacker headings.
→A line ending Klom… is a cut-off table, not a typo — copied off the screen rather than out of the message. It gets its own card.

Distributors posting their list

Post it where you
already post it.

Distributors publish their list every time it changes. Spritely reads it in the watched channels and applies it to the person who typed it — there is no "on behalf of", because a channel where a message can write to someone else's collection is a channel where anybody can.

→Two numbers, and only the first is ever zero: what changed, and what they now hold. A repost that already matches says "nothing to change" rather than "0 forms".
→The picture is drawn from what they now hold — not from what was read — so a wrong line is visible rather than something to go looking for.
→Only Distributors, only in the watched channels. The same list posted twice in a minute is read once. A trade post with a | in it is not a failed list.
